How It Works

Three Simple Steps.
That’s It.

You don’t need to know anything about property. That’s what I’m here for.

01
We Have a Chat

You reach out to Ross on WhatsApp or by phone. No formal meetings, no jargon, no pressure. Just a normal conversation about what you want to do with your money and what kind of return you’re after. Ross will be straight with you about what’s realistic.

02
We Find You the Deal

Ross goes out and finds a great below-market property in one of the UK’s strong-performing cities — Manchester, Liverpool, Leeds, Birmingham and more. He handles all the searching, negotiating, and sourcing. You just approve the deal.

03
We Refurb & You Profit

Once purchased, we manage the refurbishment and either rent the property out for ongoing income, or flip it for a profit. Either way, you receive your agreed return. Target: around 10% on cash deployed.
